# taxreport.bg Browser Extension — Privacy Policy

**Version 1.0 · Effective 19 November 2026**  
English · [Български](privacy.bg.md)

This policy covers the current **taxreport.bg browser extension**, not separately offered tax-reporting software or future website or licensing services.

**The essentials:** Tax-declaration information is processed in your browser, not uploaded to our servers. The Extension can save information to the Bulgarian National Revenue Agency (NRA) through its portal and contacts GitHub to check release/access rules.

## 1. Who provides the Extension?

**Todor Petrov**, Bulgaria, under the **taxreport.bg** product name. For privacy questions or requests: **support@taxreport.bg**.

## 2. What data is used, and why?

To help complete NRA tax declarations, the Extension processes:

- **Imported files:** filenames and tax-report data, including names, identifiers, addresses and financial/tax information.
- **NRA forms:** declaration values, edits, confirmation actions and saved declaration information, which may include fields outside the Extension’s supported sections.
- **Technical state:** relevant NRA pages/tabs, declaration context, operation status and interface preferences.

On supported NRA pages, the Extension may observe form activity **before you click Connect**. If you confirm a section directly in NRA, it may temporarily read form values and retrieve the saved declaration. It does not track your general browsing history.

Providing an imported file is optional. Without one, the Extension cannot populate declaration fields from that file; you can continue using the NRA portal directly.

## 3. Where does the data go?

- **Your browser:** File parsing, comparisons and declaration preparation take place locally. **The Extension does not upload tax files, taxpayer identifiers or declaration contents to us or taxreport.bg servers.** We do not use these data for advertising, analytics, profiling or sale.
- **NRA:** When the Extension uses the portal’s save or confirmation functions, declaration information may be sent to NRA. The Extension does not independently sign or submit a complete tax return.
- **GitHub:** Release-policy checks send **no declaration data**. GitHub receives standard connection details, such as IP address and request time. It may process them outside the European Economic Area under mechanisms such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses; see [GitHub’s privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Support:** If you email us, we use your email address and message to respond. Our email provider handles this correspondence, potentially including processing outside the EEA under applicable safeguards. Please avoid emailing sensitive tax documents.

## 4. How long is data kept?

Imported tax data, filenames, limited comparison values and workflow state are kept in **Chrome extension session storage** on your device. The Extension does not separately encrypt this data. Closing its interface or ending a connection does not clear the import. Session data is cleared when Chrome restarts or the Extension is disabled, reloaded or updated.

**Remove file** clears the imported declaration state; uninstalling removes extension-owned storage. Neither action deletes your original file or data saved with NRA. Non-tax release-policy settings may persist locally until updated or removed. Support emails are kept only as long as reasonably needed to answer enquiries and meet legal obligations.

## 5. Legal basis and your rights

Where we act as a data controller, processing necessary to provide requested functions relies on the applicable license (**GDPR Article 6(1)(b)**), insofar as it concerns your own data. Support and service protection rely on legitimate interests (**Article 6(1)(f)**); legal obligations on **Article 6(1)(c)**. If you handle another person’s declaration, you must have an appropriate legal basis and authorization.

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability, or object to processing, at **support@taxreport.bg**. We cannot access, retrieve or remotely erase declaration data stored only in your browser. You may complain to the **Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (КЗЛД)** or another competent authority.

## 6. Changes

We will update this policy if data practices change materially, including when activation codes are introduced. [English](privacy.en.md) and [Bulgarian](privacy.bg.md) versions are intended to have equivalent meaning.

**The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.**
